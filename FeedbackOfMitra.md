Good
Answer detection: Accurately understands whether the candidate has actually answered the question.
Question clarification: AI can explain and simplify the question effectively.
In depth: Listen to answers and ask from that goes in depth.
JD support: Can accept and process the Job Description.
Interview planning: Generates an interview plan based on the JD.
Listening & questioning: Good at listening and asking relevant questions.
Meeting setup: Can generate the interview meeting link.
Voice detection: Effectively focuses on the primary voice and filters background noise.

Bad

Repeat handling: If the candidate says “repeat,” it avoids repeating the question and directly jusp to the next question
Camera/Mute controls: Camera, mute, and unmute options seem unnecessary and their purpose is unclear in sense of AI interview.
Long answers: No clear handling if a candidate continues answering the same question for a very long time.
Minimized screen: No warning when the interview screen is minimized.
Transcript quotes: Admin-side quotes often highlight random statements instead of key points.
Multiple users: Multiple users may be able to join the same interview simultaneously with the same link.
Silence handling: The interview closes after around 6–7 minutes of silence instead of handling the situation conversationally.
Repeated questions: The AI sometimes asks the same question multiple times back-to-back.

Improvements

Face verification: Match the candidate's face with their registered photo.
Eye detection: Monitor face/eye presence during the interview.
Resume-JD matching: Add a minimum relevance threshold before starting the interview.
Full-screen mode: Automatically enter full-screen mode when the interview starts.
Tab monitoring: Monitor tab switching during the interview.
Background voice alerts: Notify the candidate when significant background voices are detected.
Active engagement: If the candidate is stuck or silent, the AI should prompt them or move to the next question.
Interview status: Show immediately if an interview has already been completed when the candidate enters the meeting credentials.

Non-Existing Features

Eye tracking/detection
Voice monitoring
Full-screen enforcement
Candidate authorization/identity detection
